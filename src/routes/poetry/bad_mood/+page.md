Why are you in a bad mood?  
Don't you know  
what you're saying upsets me?

Serious and rude,  
that's how you come off:  
Cold and Unfriendly.

I don't want to talk to you.  
There may have been love,  
that's in the past now.

There's nothing we can do  
It's over.

&nbsp;

No I'm not in a bad mood.  
I'm sorry,  
what did I say to upset you?

That's not what I meant to do.  
I want you to feel like you're the only person  
with whom I confide.

Please know that I want you,  
It's just: I have trouble  
sleeping and breathing

Please know that I want you.
